Customer journeys

Every way in ends the same: a paid ticket, confirmed by Star Cinemas’ own systems

Customers reach the same Sales Employee on the website, the app, a dedicated phone number, WhatsApp, or by scanning a QR code. It keeps one memory across all of them, so a conversation that starts on a call can finish on WhatsApp without repeating anything.

Website and app

  1. Opens Tap-to-Talk on the website, or the ShivAI entry point in the app.
  2. Talks or types. The screen fills in as the conversation moves.
  3. Picks the film, showtime and seats by voice or by touch.
  4. Pays on the payment gateway’s secure page.
  5. Gets the ticket and QR on screen, and a copy on WhatsApp.

Everything happens on one screen. Six screens below show the whole flow.

Phone call

  1. Calls the Star Cinemas number. The Sales Employee answers in the caller’s language.
  2. Chooses the film, showtime and number of tickets by voice.
  3. Agrees to WhatsApp. The payment link arrives while still on the call.
  4. Pays on the phone. The Sales Employee confirms it on the call.
  5. Gets the ticket and QR on WhatsApp.

Voice for talking; WhatsApp for anything that must be tapped or kept.

WhatsApp

  1. Messages the Star Cinemas WhatsApp number.
  2. Gets film cards with trailers, then a showtime list.
  3. Picks seats with a quick reply, or opens the seat map.
  4. Pays through the link in the chat.
  5. Gets the ticket and QR in the same chat, and comes back later for help.

The same chat handles complaints, lost and found and refunds in Module 2.

QR code

  1. Scans a QR on a poster, standee, counter, ticket or seat.
  2. The Sales Employee opens on the phone, no app needed, already knowing the film and cinema.
  3. Picks show, seats and snacks in a few taps or by voice.
  4. Pays on the gateway’s secure page.
  5. Gets the ticket and QR on screen and on WhatsApp.

Turns every poster and queue into a booking point.

Scope

Two modules, one AI Employee team

Module 1 earns revenue and goes live first. Module 2 saves staff time and goes live once Module 1 is stable.

Module 1 · Phase 1

Discover, recommend, sell, upsell

Goal: more completed bookings and a higher spend per customer.

  1. Understand cinema, date, language, mood, party size, anything special.
  2. Recommend two or three films with reasons; trailers on digital channels.
  3. Choose and price from the live schedule and ticket types.
  4. Seats from the live map; a hold placed by the Operations Employee.
  5. Upsell one offer from Star Cinemas’ rules; a no is accepted.
  6. Pay on the gateway’s hosted page through a link on the customer’s channel.
  7. Scan and book from QR codes on posters, counters, tickets and seats, each tracked to its location.
  8. Confirm and deliver only after the gateway and the booking system confirm; ticket and QR on screen and on WhatsApp.

Star Cinemas controls the offers, the films to promote, showtime and language priorities, the tone of voice, and which cases go to a person.

Module 2 · Phase 2

Support, resolve, manage

Goal: faster post-purchase service and refund decisions, with people in control of money.

  1. Log and sort complaints: booking, facilities and service, food and drink, lost and found, other.
  2. Route the case to the right team or cinema manager; acknowledge with a reference on WhatsApp.
  3. Assess refunds against Star Cinemas’ policy: time to showtime, ticket type, partial or full.
  4. Manager approval in one tap from the dashboard or WhatsApp, with the summary and the policy clause.
  5. Carry out the decision: refund API, refund link, or hand-over to finance.
  6. Update and close the customer in the same chat until the case is resolved.

The exact refund steps depend on what Star Cinemas’ payment and refund systems allow; confirmed during discovery.

One controlled door. The Operations Employee reaches Star Cinemas’ systems only over the integration link: TLS 1.3, mTLS or signed API keys, an IP allow-list or VPN, and a log on both sides. Star Cinemas can close it at any time.
No card data. Payment happens on the gateway’s hosted page. ShivAI sees only the payment status.
Personal data is masked in logs and in the dashboard for staff without the right role. Retention periods for recordings are set by Star Cinemas.
Isolated tenant. Star Cinemas’ data, models and keys are separated from every other ShivAI customer, with encryption at rest and a per-tenant key.

Assumptions

If discovery finds any of these untrue, we agree a change before the build starts.

  • Timeline. The delivery dates depend on Star Cinemas providing each input by the week shown. A late input moves the go-live dates by at least the same amount.
  • System access. NUVIO, the ticketing engine and the payment gateway offer documented APIs and a sandbox with test data from day one.
  • Source of truth. Seat inventory, prices, payment status and tickets stay in Star Cinemas’ systems. ShivAI keeps no copy of seat inventory.
  • Holds. The booking system can hold seats while a customer pays. If not, ShivAI books only after payment and tells the customer that seats are not held.
  • Payments. Payments happen on the gateway’s hosted page and are confirmed by webhook. ShivAI never takes card details by voice or chat.
  • WhatsApp. ShivAI has admin access to Star Cinemas’ Meta Business and WhatsApp Business accounts by the end of week 1, and a verified number by the end of week 2. Meta approves templates in 1 to 5 days each, so templates are submitted in week 2.
  • Telephony. Star Cinemas provides the dedicated number on a SIP trunk from a licensed UAE operator.
  • Languages. English and Arabic at launch. Each further language is a change request of about 2 weeks.
  • Content and people. Star Cinemas supplies and approves the knowledge base, menus, offers, refund policy and approval chain, and names a project owner, an IT contact per system and an operations manager for Module 2, each answering within 1 business day.
  • Integration link. The allow-list or VPN and sandbox credentials are in place by the end of week 1. Production credentials are issued before the pilot in week 6.
  • Data residency. Processing in a UAE or GCC cloud region meets Star Cinemas’ data rules. If Star Cinemas needs processing inside its own network, ShivAI can deploy the platform on Star Cinemas’ own servers instead.
  • Out of scope. Outbound campaigns, changes inside NUVIO or the ticketing engine, loyalty-programme integration, and features beyond the two modules.
