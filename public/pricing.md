# Pricing — ShivAI

ShivAI is an AI voice-calling platform. Pricing is usage-based rather than flat-tier: it scales with call volume, number of AI Employees, languages required, storage, and integrations.

## Free Trial
- Price: $0
- Card required: No
- Purpose: Test AI calling — voice quality, setup, and integration — before committing to a paid plan.

## Starter
- For: Small businesses testing AI calling
- Includes: 1 AI Employee, website & app integration, email support, basic voice analytics
- Price: Custom quote — based on call volume

## Growth
- For: Teams scaling call volume across channels
- Includes: Multiple AI Employees, advanced integrations (CRM, calendars, payments), priority support, custom voice branding
- Price: Custom quote — based on call volume

## Enterprise
- For: Large organizations with compliance needs
- Includes: Unlimited AI Employees, dedicated success manager, SLA, on-premises or hybrid deployment, full white-label
- Price: Custom quote

## How to get a quote
- Sign up for the free trial at https://callshivai.com and request a quote from within the product, or email hello@callshivai.com with your expected call volume and use case.
